---

## The Problem

India's retail investor base has exploded — 16+ crore demat accounts, with 70%+ from Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities. But this growth has outrun financial literacy:

### The Scale of the Crisis

- **₹12,000 Crore** lost daily to financial fraud in India
- **9 out of 10** individual F&O traders lose money (SEBI study)
- **70%+** of new demat accounts come from non-metro cities
- Scam messages flood **WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, Instagram** — in Hindi and regional languages

### Who Gets Hurt?

1. **First-time investors from Tier-2/3 cities** — drawn to "guaranteed return" schemes on social media
2. **Regional-language users** — who can't read English-heavy financial disclaimers
3. **Young investors (18-25)** — following unregistered "advisors" on YouTube/Telegram
4. **Elderly investors** — susceptible to phishing, fake SEBI calls, and Ponzi schemes
5. **Homemakers managing family savings** — targeted by WhatsApp investment groups

### The Specific Problem We Solve

**Every day, millions of Indians receive messages like:**

> "💰 Join our premium stock group! 500% returns guaranteed! SEBI registered advisor. Only ₹5,000 to join. Limited seats — join NOW!"

**Most users cannot tell if this is legitimate or a scam.** They don't know:
- How to verify if someone is actually SEBI-registered
- That guaranteed returns are a red flag
- Where to report fraud
- What their rights are as investors

**The gap:** There is no simple, WhatsApp-native, Hindi-first tool that lets a user forward a suspicious message and instantly understand if it's a scam.

---

## Why Existing Solutions Don't Work

| Existing Solution | Why It Fails |
|---|---|
| SEBI website | English-only, complex, requires knowing what to search |
| SCORES portal | Difficult to navigate, no mobile-friendly interface |
| Bank/Broker warnings | Generic, not personalized, arrive after the fact |
| Financial literacy PDFs | Nobody reads them, jargon-heavy, not interactive |
| Google search | Returns promotional content mixed with genuine information |

**What's missing:** An immediate, frictionless, vernacular-first tool that intercepts scams *before* money changes hands.
